How it works

Benchmark the decision, not just the model.

Every problem starts with an operational threshold. AI only wins when a simpler method cannot meet it.

01◎

Start with the requirement

Define accuracy, latency, cost, volume, and auditability before choosing a technology.

02≡

Compare different levels of intelligence

Rules, traditional ML, small models, and frontier LLMs compete on the same task.

03↗

Measure the whole system

A model that wins on accuracy can still lose on latency, cost, reliability, or operational complexity.

04✓

Prefer the simplest passing option

The recommendation is the lowest-complexity method that satisfies the active constraints.

Project thesis

AI should earn its complexity.

When two approaches both meet the requirement, SimplestWins favors the one that is easier to understand, cheaper to operate, faster to run, and simpler to maintain. The benchmark exists to make that tradeoff visible.
